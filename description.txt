[es-ES]
Una interfaz simple y dinamica para rastrear tus mejoras permanentes consumidas. Inspirado en Munchies - Consumables Checklist.
¡Por favor, lee la pagina de la Steam Workshop para ver la descripcion completa y todos los detalles!

A simple, clean, and dynamic interface to track your consumed permanent upgrades across Vanilla Terraria, Calamity Mod, Thorium Mod, and Mod of Redemption.

📋 Context & Credits
This mod is completely independent and was created 100% using AI assistance. The main purpose of this project is to be used as a tailored feature for the "Infernal Eclipse of Ragnarok" modpack. 

MODS
Calamity
Thorium
SOTS (Secrets of the Shadows)
Redemption
